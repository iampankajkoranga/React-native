Privacy Policy for SnookerScore

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

SnookerScore ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Pankaj Koranga.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SnookerScore mobile application. 1. Information We Collect SnookerScore does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or password. The app is primarily designed to help users record and manage snooker scores. Some information may be collected automatically by third-party services used by the app, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). 2. Advertising SnookerScore uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect certain information from your device and interactions with the app for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and improving the advertising service. Depending on Google's services and your device settings, this may include: IP address Device or advertising identifiers App and user interaction information Diagnostic and performance information General information about the device Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's privacy practices. Consent and Personalized Ads Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in some US states, are shown a consent message when they first open the app. It is provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept, decline or manage your choices. If you decline, the app shows non-personalized ads. Where this message is shown, you can change your choice at any time using the "Privacy" button on the app's home screen. Users in other regions are not shown this message. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). 3. Local Data Storage SnookerScore may store certain app-related information locally on your device to provide and maintain app functionality. This information is not used to identify you personally and is not transmitted to us as personal information. 4. How We Use Information Information handled by the app and its third-party services may be used to: Provide and maintain app functionality Display advertisements Improve app performance and reliability Prevent fraud and abuse Understand general app and advertising interactions 5. Data Sharing SnookerScore does not sell users' personal information. Information may be collected or shared by third-party services integrated into the app, such as Google Mobile Ads, according to their respective privacy policies and services. 6. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. Data transmitted by Google's Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS, according to Google's documentation. 7. Data Retention and Deletion SnookerScore does not maintain user accounts or a personal user database. Information stored locally by the app remains on the user's device and can generally be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through the device settings. Data handled by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is subject to their respective data retention and privacy policies. 8. Children's Privacy SnookerScore is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable age of consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. 9. Third-Party Services SnookerScore uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which is operated by Google. Google's services may collect and process information as described in their policies. 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date of this policy. 11.
